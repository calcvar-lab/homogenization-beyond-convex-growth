# Dependency registry — `Homogenization_beyond_convex_growthV1.tex`

This registry maps the numbered/labelled mathematical results of the paper to the Lean
declarations. It covers the `definition`, `proposition`, `lemma`, `theorem` and `example`
environments active in the source file. Remarks, internal equations and commented-out labels are not
result rows.

**Statuses.** `proved` means that a Lean proof exists for the formalized statement. That proof may
be conditional on the explicit arguments of its type: this does not formalize the results supplied
as hypotheses. `cited hypothesis` means that a result from another paper is represented by a
`def ... : Prop` supplied to the theorem. `not formalized` means that no complete Lean proof of the
stated result is present. Partially formalized results are detailed in the Lean column.

The dependencies list the main direct mathematical/formal dependencies, not the whole tree of Lean
imports. The cost estimates the remaining work needed to fully formalize the statement (or, for
`proved`, the cost of completing the cited results it depends on), in person-days. These estimates
are indicative; the cited results of functional analysis may require substantial formalization.

### Detailed status of `prop:properdomain`

The passage of the paper (subsection `properdomain`, `Homogenization_beyond_convex_growth.tex`,
statement `prop:properdomain`) fixes `m=m₁+m₂` and identifies a stacked matrix
`ζ∈Mat(m,d)` with the pair of its first `m₁` rows and its last `m₂` rows.
The paper then sets `W♯(x,ξ)=W_v(x,ξ¹)+G₂(ξ²)` and
`G♯(ξ)=G(ξ¹)+G₂(ξ²)`, with `G` defined by (Dg), `W_v` by (sepW), and `G₂` by (DG2).
The clauses of the proposition are: (i) convexity, lower semicontinuity and geometry of
`dom G♯`; (ii) `dom W♯(x,·)=Σ×C₂`; (iii) `Ytloc`/`Zaff` identities and growth estimates;
(iv) hypotheses H1–H8 and homogenization formula.

Status of the objects and results, by kind:

- **General form proved**: [`ProperDomainGeneral.lean`](Homogenization/Examples/ProperDomainGeneral.lean) states (DG2) as the structure `ProperDomainG2Data` (`G₂` convex, l.s.c., `G₂ ≥ |·|_F^p`, `C₂ = dom G₂` open, unbounded, containing `0`, different from the whole space, `G₂(tχ) → ∞` as `t → 1⁻` for `χ ∈ ∂C₂`) and proves clauses (i)–(iv) for every such `G₂`: `properDomainGSharp_pd_i`, `properDomainWSharp_pd_ii`, `properDomainWSharp_pd_iii` (upper bound for every `β` of `sep:iii`, with `β♯ = max(β,1)`), `properDomainWSharp_hypotheses`, `properDomainWSharp_pd_iv`. (H8) comes from `prop_Z3suff_paper`, which requires the radial blow-up on `∂(dom G♯)` only. The half-space example is `properDomainG2Data_example`. The items below describe the direct proof for this example.

## Scope of the main proof

`main_Ggrowth_final` has a Lean proof, but its type takes four propositions as arguments
(`CitedAHM14`, `CitedAffineApprox`, `CitedZaffLocLip` and `CitedVitaliEnvelope`). The
third is now proved (`citedZaffLocLip`, in `RankOne.lean`), and `main_Ggrowth_final_three` is the
same theorem with the three hypotheses `CitedAHM14`, `CitedAffineApprox` and `CitedVitaliEnvelope`.
Next, `step4` only requires the inequality form `VitaliEnvelopeUpper` of the Vitali theorem, proved
in `Vitali.lean` (`vitaliEnvelopeUpper`), hence `main_Ggrowth_final_two`, which only assumes
